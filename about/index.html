About

I research the genetics and evolution of plants.

I’m currently a data scientist at Vylor, where I develop new models and data sources for plant breeding as a member of the Genomic Data Science team. I was previously a postdoc working with Peter Balint-Kurti at North Carolina State University. I completed my PhD in the Population Biology Graduate Group at UC Davis where I worked with Jeffrey Ross-Ibarra.
